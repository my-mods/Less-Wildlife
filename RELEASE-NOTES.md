# Less Wildlife 0.0.0

- Set ordinary boar and wolf populations from 10% to 200%. 100% is the normal population and default; positive populations retain at least one animal.
- Independently turn Adjust boars and Adjust wolves On or Off through Mod Settings.
- Restore owned original population values on later encounters when a species adjustment is Off.
- Increase populations up to twice their original size, including groups starting with one animal.
- Share one bounded population hook and cache, with optional aggregated Logging.
